## 1. Standard and scope

Guesto commits to conforming with the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1
at level AA** across the entire user-facing surface of the platform. The standard is
enforced by the design system, by automated axe checks in the CI pipeline, and by manual
review against the applicable success criteria before every release. The relevant European
harmonised standard is EN 301 549 v3.2.1, which incorporates WCAG 2.1 AA by reference; the
Guesto conformance target is therefore aligned with the European Accessibility Act (Directive
(EU) 2019/882) as it is transposed into the Member State law applicable at the seat of the
operator.

## 2. Conformance status by surface

- **Public menu (`/r/[slug]` and `/r/[slug]/[locale]`):** semantic markup, `<html lang>` set
  per locale, no JavaScript required, allergen and dietary information carried in text (never
  by colour alone), touch targets ≥ 44 × 44 px, focus indicators visible at 3:1 contrast
  against the background. Public pages are the surface a restaurant guest sees and are the
  strictest of the conformance targets — they are held to WCAG 2.1 AA with no known
  exceptions at the time of writing.
- **Dashboard (`/`, `/menus`, `/settings`, `/billing`):** targeting WCAG 2.1 AA. Automated
  axe checks land in CI with the P1-25 task; manual review of the editor covers keyboard
  navigation, screen-reader labels on custom inputs, and the drag-and-drop reorder handles
  which fall back to keyboard-only reorder buttons.
- **Marketing site and legal pages (this document):** WCAG 2.1 AA; verified by axe on every
  merge to `main`.

## 3. Known limitations

The following are known partial conformances at the date of this statement. Placeholder —
counsel and the accessibility auditor confirm this list before release:

- The QR-code image on the print sheet is decorative and marked with an empty `alt`; the
  human-readable venue slug is printed below it as the accessible alternative.
- The colour-picker in the branding editor exposes contrast information but does not yet
  offer an accessible-name suggestion — noted as a follow-up (P1-24).
- Localised menu content is only as accessible as the source content the tenant provides; a
  tenant that writes in ALL CAPS or hides ingredients in image-only fields can produce a page
  that will fail on its content even though the surrounding shell is compliant.

## 5. Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared using the self-evaluation method described in Commission
Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1523 for public-sector bodies and adapted to the private
context of Guesto. The statement is reviewed at least once per year and re-issued whenever
a material change to the user interface would affect the conformance status.

## 6. Feedback and contact procedure

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on any Guesto page, please write to
accessibility@elvoria.local (placeholder — counsel replaces with the monitored inbox at
release) with (a) the URL where the barrier occurred, (b) the assistive technology and
browser you were using, and (c) a short description of what you expected and what actually
happened. We aim to acknowledge feedback within two business days and to propose a resolution
or an accessible alternative within 15 working days.
